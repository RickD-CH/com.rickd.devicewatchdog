Device Watchdog keeps an eye on all your Homey devices, so you don't have to. It tracks low battery, devices that have stopped sending new data, and devices Homey itself reports as unreachable - all in one place, with sensible defaults so nothing needs to be configured to get started.

Every device is monitored automatically; you can exclude individual devices or entire zones with a click, and fine-tune battery and reporting thresholds per device where needed. A manual reachability test, a running history log, optional Homey Timeline notifications, and a virtual status device for your Insights and dashboards round out the picture, alongside a full set of Flow cards for building automations around what it finds.
